Click inside enclosed background pockets — e.g. inside letters like O, D, R — to clear trapped background the edge flood-fill can't reach.

01 Colour Remover

Pick a colour (or sample it straight off the image) and strip out every matching pixel across the whole canvas — not just a connected region, unlike Remove Background above — useful for wiping out a specific ink colour (e.g. all black linework) wherever it appears.

02 Crop

Removing a background leaves the empty space around it in place — the canvas stays the same size, just with that area now see-through. That wastes room on a gang sheet and makes the artwork harder to place. Trim it away here.

Or draw your own crop box — useful for cutting out something unwanted, or cropping a picture that still has its background.

03 Touch-up brush

Manual trace override brush.

Apply the selected texture across the whole design in one pass, at the density set above. Only erodes pixels that already have ink — safe to run more than once to build up more texture.

04 Upscale (AI)

Real AI reconstruction (same engine as the Upscale tool) — runs fully offline, no blanket resize.

05 Resize to spec

If the crop cuts off part of the design, nudge it with these before applying — nothing is touched until you hit Apply.

06 Targeted Halftone

Pick exactly which colours to halftone — each gets its own dot pitch and match tolerance. Untouched colours stay pixel-perfect.

Turn this on and click anywhere on the image to add that exact pixel's colour as its own swatch.

07 Colour Adjust
